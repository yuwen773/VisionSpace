# User center — surface brief

Scope: `/user/center` and `components/user-center/*`. Mode: **Operate** (the visitor is in a task), executed in the established award-tier editorial language of Landing/Explore.

Audience & job: signed-in creator checking their own archive and managing the account behind it. Primary job: see what they have made and how it is doing; secondary: edit identity, redeem VIP, control AI memory, send feedback. Frequency: periodic, not daily. Constraints: five existing sections and every existing API must survive; both themes; mobile 320px; keyboard and reduced motion.

Chosen direction: Composition **A — editorial archive** (user-selected), with recent work and the three measurements promoted into the masthead (approved). Memorable moment: the numbered index rail whose marker travels between sections, beside a staggered contact sheet of the user's own recent plates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The user center is the owner's archive, not a settings dashboard; it refuses the glass hero + rounded sidebar card that category dashboards default to. OWN-WORLD: near-black paper `#05060c`, warm ink `#f4f1ea`, violet `#b7a2ff`; light: paper `#faf7ff`, ink `#1e1b4b`, violet `#9333ea`; Noto Serif SC display, Noto Sans SC UI, JetBrains Mono for measurements; hairline rules; photographs are the only saturated color; no glass, no gradient text, no icons standing in for images. STORY: the visitor sees whose archive this is and what is inside it, then edits identity, redeems membership, tunes AI memory, or writes feedback without leaving the page. FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-width masthead — huge serif display name with a ruled ledger of role/member-since/archive-id and three tabular measurements on the left; a staggered six-plate contact sheet of recent uploads on the right; beneath, a sticky numbered index rail (01–05) on the left and the active record on the right. FORM: composition A, first on the ordered list; seed: none (pinned) "A 档案编辑式" / "数据前置为封面". FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
